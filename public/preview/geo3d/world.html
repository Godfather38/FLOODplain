Water is the SFINCS model's water surface from saved runs with forecast inputs from November 1, 2026: not current conditions. Rain is the run's own rainfall input. Ground, water, roads, bridges and buildings all sit on the model's own 10 m lidar terrain. Heights are drawn 2× taller.

    
Road and bridge-deck heights: USGS 3DEP lidar, every 5 m along the routes. HAND is a separate terrain-height estimate of where river water could reach for the 13Z run's NWM flows; river flow is each run's own inflow at the model's inlets.

    
Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, via OpenFreeMap and OpenMapTiles. Terrain: USGS Pima County 2021 lidar as resampled for the SFINCS case; Terrain Tiles (Mapzen, AWS Open Data) at the rectangle's edges. Satellite: Esri World Imagery.
