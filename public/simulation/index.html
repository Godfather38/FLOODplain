Water balance

Infiltration is a constant rainfall loss. After the storm, a 180-minute dry tail drains the network. Pits retain water. Storage is model volume, not flood depth.


Sources, assumptions and next validation
Elevation: recorded USGS 3DEP point samples. This 250 m sampling is much coarser than the source DEM and cannot resolve channels or road embankments. The window is clipped and has no external upstream inflow. Drainage uses eight neighboring cells; unresolved flats and pits are retained. A fixed exponential reservoir travel time per cell is an uncalibrated scenario parameter.
Default rain is invented. Imported MRMS PrecipRate is a radar estimate, with beam blockage, sampling and precipitation-conversion uncertainty. The multiplier explores sensitivity; it is not a confidence interval. Imported data are replayed historically, never labeled live.
Before interpreting a crossing: delineate the full basin with a conditioned DEM, verify culverts and road elevations, compare radar rain with Pima ALERT gauges, and calibrate and independently validate event hydrographs.
