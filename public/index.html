Hobby tool, not an official warning. Never drive into water crossing the road: Turn Around, Don't Drown. Arizona can bill you for your rescue if you go around a barricade.
How to use this
  1. Open it before you leave. It checks river gauges, county rain gauges, county barricades and weather warnings. It rechecks every 5 minutes while open; tap Check now to force it.
  2. Read the big box. It names the route with the lowest risk right now and how long it takes.
  3. Read the lines under it. They say why a faster route was skipped, and what to watch for on yours.
  4. Tap Open in Google Maps. Google Maps opens with stops that keep you on that route. Drive with Google as usual.
  5. Want details? Scroll to All routes. Every spot shows the county's barricade status, rain in inches, and river flow, side by side. Tap a route to zoom the map to it; tap a dot on the map for that spot.
  6. First time only: put your work address in Settings so Google Maps goes all the way there. The USGS key is optional.
  7. On your phone: use your browser's Share → Add to Home Screen so it opens like an app.

The app can be wrong or have missing data. What you see at the crossing wins. Never drive into water crossing the road.
