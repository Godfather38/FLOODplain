FLOODplain Privacy Policy & SMS Terms

Updated November 6, 2026. FLOODplain (formerly Wash Watch) is a free, non-commercial road-flooding information project for Avra Valley and Picture Rocks, Arizona.

Privacy Policy · Terms of Service · Support · Alert settings

Privacy Policy

Information we collect and use

Text signup stores your phone number, area, selected times and days, alert mode, optional access key, verification code and its time, verification status, signup date, consent version and date when available, and sending and rate-limit records. The waitlist stores your phone number, area, signup date, consent version when available, and whether its one notification was sent. We use this information to verify signup, deliver requested messages, manage preferences and prevent abuse.

Storage, delivery providers and sharing

Subscriber records are kept in a private Google Sheet controlled by the project operator, and processed by Google Apps Script. During the Verizon beta, Google email and Verizon's email-to-text gateway process the number and message. After toll-free approval and activation, Twilio and mobile carriers process numbers, message content and delivery records to deliver texts.

We do not sell mobile numbers or use them for marketing. Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information won’t be shared with any third parties. SMS opt-in data and consent are not made available for third parties' or affiliates' own purposes. Service providers process the information described above only as needed to operate and deliver the requested messaging service.

Stopping and deletion

Use Stop texts to remove your subscriber and waitlist rows from the active sheet. Replying STOP to the toll-free sender blocks future messages through that sender; it does not itself delete the app's stored rows. Providers may retain their own delivery or suppression records under their policies. Contact the operator through Support for privacy questions or deletion requests.

Other app information

Preferences and cached checks are saved in your browser. Push notifications use ntfy topics and selected schedules. Support requests, including your contact details and message, are emailed to the operator through Google. Crossing reports are stored in a private sheet and emailed to the operator when the service is connected; the fallback notification channel is public, so do not include personal information in crossing reports.

Diagnostics record device/browser details, screen size, navigation and errors, and may be posted to a public ntfy diagnostics channel. Do not include private information in URLs or crossing reports. The app also sends aggregate visit and failure counts to its service. Website, map, data and notification providers receive network requests needed to operate the app.

Terms of Service — FLOODplain texts

Messages and frequency

FLOODplain sends road-flooding information for your selected area, at your selected Arizona times and days. The default limit is 2 scheduled alerts per day; an access key can allow up to 4. Choosing “Only when something's off” can reduce that frequency. Signup also sends a requested verification code and one confirmation; code requests are limited to 3 per number per day. The optional waitlist sends one availability notification, with no recurring alerts unless you separately sign up. Service notices and responses to your requests may also be sent. No marketing messages.

Your choice

On the SMS signup form, tapping “Text me a code” agrees to the disclosed recurring alert program and requests a code. Enter the 4-digit code within 10 minutes and tap “Start texts” to activate alerts. Tapping “Join the waitlist” separately agrees to one availability text. Neither choice is required to use the free app or its maps.

Cost, cancellation and help

FLOODplain does not charge for alerts. Message and data rates may apply. For toll-free texts, reply STOP to cancel or HELP for help. After a toll-free STOP, only START or UNSTOP sent to that sender removes the carrier-level block; signing up on the website alone does not remove it. You must still complete app signup to receive recurring alerts.

During the Verizon email-to-text beta, reply commands are not monitored: use Stop texts or Support. These web controls also work for toll-free subscribers and waitlist entries. Support requests go to the person who runs FLOODplain; a HELP reply is not a promise of direct contact with that person.

Availability and limitations

All-carrier toll-free messaging remains unavailable until the sender is verified and activated. Messages may be delayed or undelivered; carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Ratings use provisional public data and model predictions and may be late or wrong. What you see at the crossing comes first. Never drive into water crossing the road.
