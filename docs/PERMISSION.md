# Getting permission for sensors

## Where sensors would go, and who says yes
| Spot | Why a sensor | Whose land | Who to ask |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Los Robles Wash bridge on Trico Rd** | 3 of 4 routes cross it, and there's no gauge for ~10 miles upstream | Road right-of-way (Pima County, to confirm) | Pima County DOT for a **Right-of-Way Use Permit**; Flood Control District as a courtesy |
| **Brawley Wash dip on Avra Valley Rd** | Most dangerous spot, and the county already barricades here | Right-of-way at the road; private land beside it | A neighbor's land is easiest (written OK). Inside the floodplain, ask Flood Control whether a **Floodplain Use Permit** is needed |
| Dips near El Paso Gas Rd and Clayton Rd | Small local washes; rain gauges cover them only roughly | Same as above | Same as above |

The county says encroachments into its right-of-way can need a Right-of-Way Use Permit from DOT, and a Floodplain Use Permit from the Flood Control District. Confirm which agency maintains each road first.

## Best first ask: have the county do it
The Regional Flood Control District already runs 100+ ALERT sensors. A resident pointing at a barricaded crossing with no stream sensor nearby is exactly the kind of request they act on. If they add one, it shows up in this app automatically, because the app already reads their feed. Flood Control's main line, from pima.gov/1427: **520-724-4600**.

## Email 1: Flood Control District (send this first)
> **Subject:** Resident request: stream sensor at Los Robles Wash / Trico Rd and Brawley Wash / Avra Valley Rd
>
> Hello,
>
> I live in Avra Valley and drive to I-10 every day. I've put together a free, non-commercial page for my household that combines your ALERT rain and stream data, the county road-closure points, and USGS gauges to pick the safest route in the morning.
>
> Two questions:
>
> 1. **Gaps:** there's no stream sensor between Brawley Wash at Milewide Rd and the crossings we drive: the Los Robles Wash bridge on Trico Rd (between El Tiro and Silverbell) and the Brawley Wash crossing on Avra Valley Rd (your barricade point "Avra Valley Rd west of the Brawley Wash"). Would the District consider an ALERT stream sensor at either one? If it helps, I'm happy to report what I see at these crossings.
> 2. **Data use:** my page reads the public ALERT map feed every ~10 minutes. Is that OK, or is there a preferred feed or terms I should follow?
>
> If the District won't place a sensor, could I install a small battery ultrasonic water-level sensor myself, on private land beside the Avra Valley Rd crossing (with the owner's permission)? Would that need a Floodplain Use Permit?
>
> Thank you,
> [your name] · [phone]

## Email 2: Pima County DOT (only if Flood Control says no)

>

>
> Thank you,
> [your name] · [phone]

> Signed / date

## Before you install anything
- Never put a post *in* the wash channel. That's where floodplain and waterway permits kick in.
- Never attach anything to traffic signs or signals.
- Keep a photo of the spot and the written OK.
